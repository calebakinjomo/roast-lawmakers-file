# The Long Roast: Lawmakers and the File They Cannot Convert

A make.fun bounty entry: one satirical sentence roasting the lawmakers who want to regulate AI, crypto and the internet — while they can't even convert a file to PDF.

> The senator drafting the bill to rein in artificial intelligence just emailed his aide a photo of his own monitor and asked her to download the PDF from the printer.

## How to run

```bash
python3 main.py
```

This prints the entry line to stdout and writes it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`).
